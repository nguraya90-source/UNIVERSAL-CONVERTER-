Why You Should Use WebP Format for Your Website

Published: July 2026 • 4 min read

In the modern digital landscape, website speed is everything. If your pages take more than three seconds to load, visitors will leave, and your search engine rankings will drop. One of the biggest culprits behind slow load times is unoptimized images.

For decades, JPEG and PNG were the standard image formats. However, Google introduced a modern alternative called WebP that changes the game entirely.

What is WebP?

WebP is a modern image format that provides superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the web. Developed by Google, it was designed specifically to create smaller, richer images that make the web faster.

The Major Benefits of WebP

  • Smaller File Sizes: WebP lossy images are visually identical to JPEGs but are generally 25% to 34% smaller in file size.
  • Retained Quality: Even when compressed to drastically lower sizes, WebP retains exceptional crispness and handles colors beautifully.
  • Transparency Support: Unlike JPEGs, WebP supports transparent backgrounds, making it a perfect replacement for heavy PNG files.
